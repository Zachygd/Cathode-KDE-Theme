# Changelog

## 0.6.2

First public alpha.

- **Windows 98 look for KDE Plasma 6**: 3D window frames, gray 98 taskbar and menus, pushed-in task buttons, yellow tooltips, Windows widget style, Wine Tahoma 9pt.
- **Cathode Start**: Plasma's Application Menu with a Start button that pushes in while open, a vertical Cathode banner, and apps-only search.
- **Cathode Clock**: the sunken 98 notification-area box around the clock and 16px tray icons.
- **Lock screen and Shut Down dialog** in 98 style, with Sleep, Log Off and Shut Down on the lock screen.
- **16 colour schemes**, each light and dark: Cathode, Brick, Pumpkin, Wheat, Ember, Spruce, Meadow, Marine, Teal, Plum, Eggplant, Rose, Storm, Slate, Spectrum and Sunset. Title bars, selections, Start button, banner, logo, desktop colour, and the lock and Shut Down screens all follow the scheme.
- **Display Properties** app: pick a light and a dark scheme, then use Light, Dark or Automatic (KDE switches at sunrise and sunset). The Brightness and Color dark mode switch flips between your two. Choose solid colour or wallpaper.
- SE98 icons with the Cathode logo, ModernXP cursors, KDE 3 sounds, animated login splash and optional Plymouth boot animation.
- Self-extracting installer (`Cathode-Installer.run`) and a one-line `get.sh` installer.
