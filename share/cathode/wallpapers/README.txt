Cathode wallpapers
==================

Put a wallpaper for a colour scheme here and Display Properties will offer it:

    <scheme>.png        used for light and dark (also .jpg, .jpeg, .webp)
    <scheme>-dark.png   used for the dark version instead, if present

Scheme names: cathode brick pumpkin wheat ember spruce meadow marine teal
              plum eggplant rose storm slate spectrum sunset

Until a scheme has a wallpaper, its "Wallpaper" option stays greyed out and
the desktop uses the scheme's solid colour.
